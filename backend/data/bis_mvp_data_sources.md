# BIS MVP Knowledge Base Data Sources

This directory contains the curated Indian Standards MVP Knowledge Base (`bis_mvp_standards.json`) compiled from official Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) publications, Product Manuals, Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and Gazette Notifications.

## Data Sources & Citation Index

1. **Self-Ballasted LED Lamps & Luminaires (STD001 - STD009)**
   - **Source**: Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Official Document: *Annexure-2 – List of Major Consumer Products Notified for Compulsory Marking*
   - **URL**: [https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/temp_2131689472.pdf](https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/temp_2131689472.pdf)
   - **Notified Framework**: Electronics and Information Technology Goods (Compulsory Registration) Scheme.

2. **Industrial Safety Helmets (STD010)**
   - **Source**: BIS Product Manual for Industrial Safety Helmets (`PM/IS 2925/1/January 2019`)
   - **URL**: [https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/IS-2925-Product-Manual.pdf](https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/IS-2925-Product-Manual.pdf)

3. **High Ankle Tactical Boots (STD011)**
   - **Source**: BIS Product Manual for High Ankle Tactical Boots with PU-Rubber Sole (`PM/IS 17012/1/March 2021`)
   - **URL**: [https://bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/PM_IS_17012.pdf](https://bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/PM_IS_17012.pdf)

4. **Compendium of Indian Standards on Cement (STD012 - STD020)**
   - **Source**: BIS Official *Compendium of Indian Standards on Cement* (Civil Engineering Department)
   - **URL**: [https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/COMPENDIUM-OF-CEMENT-STANDARDS.pdf](https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/COMPENDIUM-OF-CEMENT-STANDARDS.pdf)
   - **Regulatory Order**: Covered under the Cement (Quality Control) Order, 2003 (DPIIT).

5. **PVC Insulated Electric Cables (STD021)**
   - **Source**: BIS Product Manual for PVC Insulated Cables/Cords (`PM/IS 694/3/May 2020`)
   - **URL**: [https://bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Product-Manual-694-V3.pdf](https://bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Product-Manual-694-V3.pdf)

6. **Solar DC & Fire Survival Cables (STD022 - STD023)**
   - **Source**: Official Gazette of India Notification: *Solar DC Cable and Fire Survival Cable (Quality Control) Order, 2023*
   - **URL**: [https://bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Solar-DC-Cable-and-Fire-Survival-Cable-QCO-2023.pdf](https://bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Solar-DC-Cable-and-Fire-Survival-Cable-QCO-2023.pdf)
